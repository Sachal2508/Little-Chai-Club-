Privacy Notice

Little Chai Club is a plush toy brand inspired by South Asia. This page explains what happens to your email address when you sign up on our launch page.

What we collect

Only your email address and the date and time you signed up. We don't use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics on this page.

Why we collect it

To email you when our first plush collection launches, and with occasional news about Little Chai Club. We don't sell or share your email address for anyone else's marketing.

Where it is kept

Signups are stored in a private spreadsheet that only the Little Chai Club team can open. The page is hosted by Vercel and signups are stored with Google, which process data on our behalf. These providers may keep standard server logs.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we have finished contacting people about the launch, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your email address, or stop receiving emails, at any time by writing to us.
