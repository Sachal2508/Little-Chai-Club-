a note from our founder

Why I started Little Chai Club

Growing up, 4:00 PM teatime was never just about having a warm drink. It was the daily ritual that stopped the clock. It meant family gathered around the kitchen table, dipping crispy rusks into sweetened chai, snacking on crumbly nan khatai, and laughing over the smallest stories.

"We wanted to turn that feeling of warmth, comfort, and nostalgic cultural pride into something tangible — something you can hold, squeeze, and cherish every single day."

Every plushie is designed with deep care for authentic textures, custom velboa fabrics, child-safe embroidered faces, and joyful storytelling. Thank you for welcoming our tiny friends into your homes.

☕ Inspired by Tradition

Every character in our club takes inspiration from beloved South Asian teatime staples. From crunchy rusks ready for dunking to delicate nan khatai cookies, sweet swirl buns, and fiery hari mirch with a gentle heart.

🪡 Handcrafted with Care

Our plushies are created in thoughtful small batches. Each piece features custom ultra-soft velboa, child-safe embroidered expressions, and reinforced seams made to withstand years of hugs and imaginative play.

💛 Collectible & Gifting

Whether you are introducing young ones to cultural heritage or looking for a comforting desk buddy that sparks joy, Little Chai Club friends bring warmth, humor, and connection to every corner of your home.

Made for comfort, cuddles, and community.

We believe that toys should carry soul, story, and warmth. In a busy world, teatime represents a moment to pause, savor, and share. Our soft toys are a huggable reminder that comfort is always nearby.
